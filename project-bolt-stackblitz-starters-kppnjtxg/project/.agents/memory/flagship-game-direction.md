---
name: Flagship game direction
description: Product scope for the arcade's first signature game.
---

Focus JB PLAYNET BETA on one standout game at a time rather than expanding a catalog of familiar classics. The flagship is a compact, approachable action-adventure/RPG, not a large Zelda-scale project. The user wants combat-earned randomized gear drops with rarity tiers from common to legendary and chose short push-your-luck expeditions: secure gear in safe rooms or risk carrying it deeper. The first test is desktop keyboard-and-mouse, single-player, and about five minutes. The user is leaning toward pixelated hack-and-slash with top-down clarity and depth cues; full 3D scope is a concern. The setting is non-fantasy: an unknown buried complex is the central story mystery. The opening follows a missing surveyor or strange signal, then the entrance seals behind the new adventurer. They chose to test both a moving guard and a dodge.

**Why:** The user wants one game that stays fun and feels distinctive, and sees a large catalog of familiar titles as a weaker priority. Enemy drops can reward combat, while choosing to secure or risk loot adds tension. The user values a strong opening and explicitly does not want a fantasy world. Search found design analysis, but no reliable universal player-preference survey for dodge versus block; both are being tested rather than treated as a universal preference.

**How to apply:** Keep the first prototype small and playable. Make random loot exciting without making rare drops mandatory; bank gear at safe rooms and preserve secured gear on defeat. Build desktop-first controls with quick attacks, moving guard, and dodge; test the feel before adding complexity. Prefer a 2D pixel-art prototype with layered depth cues before committing to full 3D. Keep future game additions behind feedback on this flagship rather than growing the catalog.
